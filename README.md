# EnergyPlus

This GradientsGTM project provides the modeling of increase in stable ground temperatures with deep depth (>15m). 

EnergyPlus users can enter geological thermal knowledge of the ground that surrounds Vertical Ground Heat Exchangers (VGHE) with linear gradients (K/m) in extensible number of segments (upper and lower depths) to the full depth (H meters) of the borehole. 

Melbourne University recorded measurements down to 50m will be used in models with GradientsGTM. 
The simulated results will be analysed with reference to simulation results without GradientsGTM. 

As the ground temperature increases with depth modeling with GradientsGTM will show increase in performance of VGHE for winter heating. GradientsGTM will give enhanced performance to (1) get more precise designs of VGHE depth, thus contribute to the reduction in borehole drilling cost, 
   (2) predict the life of ground coupled heat pumps for sustainable winter heating
